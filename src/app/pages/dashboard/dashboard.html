Welcome to the Smart Campus Lost & Found Management System

Report, search, claim, and manage lost or found items easily across campus!

What You Can Do

Report Items Quickly report lost or found items with details, images, and location information. Search Items Search items by category, location, date, or description to find matches faster. Claim Items Submit a claim request and verify ownership before receiving the item.

Admin Dashboard

Manage Items View, update, approve, or remove lost and found item records. Manage Users Monitor registered users and manage campus lost and found access. Manage Claims Review claim requests and verify item ownership before approval.
